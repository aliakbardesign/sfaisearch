About this prototype
  • AI Overview: the one-sentence summary is generic and adapts to the search type (category, comparison, alternatives, product, price, free plan). Every rating, review count, price and date comes from the product's page on softwarefinder.com (captured Oct 20, 2026). Product one-liners are condensed from those pages.
  • "Custom pricing" means the product page has no pricing section. No price was made up.
  • Product links in the overview: clicking anywhere on a product row opens the product's page on softwarefinder.com in a new tab. All 63 product URLs in this prototype were checked against the live site on Oct 20, 2026.
  • Existing search results (counts, tabs, cards) are copied from softwarefinder.com/search-result for the matched keyword.
  • Sample content: the suggested questions and the question-to-topic matching. Homepage star ratings in "Choose from 100,000+" are approximate. LMS tab logos are placeholders.
  • Logos and images load from Software Finder's CDN, so you need to be online to see them.
